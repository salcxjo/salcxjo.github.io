classical  ·  شعر کلاسیک
Hafez  ·  حافظ 14th c. · Shiraz
الا یا أیها الساقی أدر کأساً و ناولها که عشق آسان نمود اول ولی افتاد مشکل‌ها
O cupbearer, pass around the wine and offer the cup — for love seemed easy at first, but then came the difficulties.
✦ the opening of the Divan. every time i read it i think about how the hard part always reveals itself after you've already committed to something. also the word مشکل‌ها lands different when you've had a rough month.
Rumi  ·  مولانا 13th c. · Balkh / Konya
بشنو این نی چون شکایت می‌کند از جدایی‌ها حکایت می‌کند
Listen to the reed — how it tells a tale of separations, complaining of its longing.
✦ first two lines of the Masnavi. the reed cut from the reed bed. something about being far from origin keeps pulling me back here — not in a sad way, more like an honest way.
modern  ·  معاصر
Forough Farrokhzad  ·  فروغ فرخزاد 20th c. · Tehran
I will plant my hands in the garden, I will grow, I know, I know, I know. And swallows will lay their eggs in the hollow of my ink-stained fingers.
✦ from "Another Birth." she writes the body as landscape and the landscape as body. very grounding when i feel scattered into too many things at once.
Ahmad Shamloo  ·  احمد شاملو 20th c. · Tehran
من در این زمان که سرشارم از شعر با تو سخن می‌گویم
In this moment, brimming with poetry, I am speaking to you.
✦ shamloo's free verse always feels like someone speaking directly into your chest. will add more of his work here — this is just a placeholder for now.

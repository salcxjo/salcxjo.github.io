✨ WHO IS THIS GUY? ✨

A place to gather fractions of me — part of what makes whole.


In a world of specialization, I am left behind with all my interests. I have many. Rarely wanting to master them. I want to experience the riches of life.


Job hunting tried to convince me my worth = my offer letter. This page is my answer to that. I am a software nerd AND a kayaker AND someone who thinks about poetry at weird hours AND a person who respects people who work with their hands. None of these cancel out the others.
